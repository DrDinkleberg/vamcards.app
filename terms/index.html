2. What VAM does — and what it does not do

VAM records transactions that have already happened between you and another person, in cash, in the physical world. It is a ledger, a stock list, and a way to talk to the other vendors working the same show.

VAM is not a marketplace. No payment is taken through the App for cards. There is no checkout, no cart, no order, and no payment processor in it. Nothing is shipped. An “ask price” you publish to an event floor is information for another vendor standing in the same hall — it is not an offer capable of acceptance through the App, and we are not a party to, broker of, or escrow for any deal you strike.

Every transaction you make with another person is between you and that person. You are responsible for the goods you sell, the goods you buy, their authenticity, their grading and condition, your own pricing, and your own taxes. We are not.

6. Prices, price data, and the absence of any guarantee

Read this section before you rely on a number in this App to buy a card.

VAM displays no third-party market price data. There is no price feed in this App. Every number you see is one of two things:

  1. An ask price — typed by the vendor offering the card, whatever they feel like asking. We do not check it.
  2. A realized price statistic — an aggregate of what VAM users reported that they actually sold that card for, at that event, that day.

Realized prices are user-reported. We do not verify them, and we do not guarantee them. They are self-reported by vendors, entered by hand, subject to typos, subject to condition and grading differences we do not capture, drawn from a small and non-random sample, and deliberately aggregated and suppressed for privacy — we show a median and a range only, and we show nothing at all below three independent observations. A VAM price statistic is not an appraisal, not a valuation, not a buylist, not investment advice, and not a representation that any card can be bought or sold at that price.

You alone decide what to pay and what to charge. Any loss you take on a transaction is yours. To the fullest extent the law allows, we disclaim all liability for decisions made in reliance on any number displayed in the App.

Card names, set names, set codes and collector numbers are used nominatively — to identify which card you are holding — and are the trademarks of their respective owners. VAM is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any game publisher. We ship no publisher card art. Publisher-specific notices, including the one Riot Games requires, are in the Privacy Policy and in the App under Account → Legal & Safety.
